What is running here?
Everything runs in your browser. Payloads are unsigned examples, and HTTP messages are simulated—not responses from live authorization or resource servers. No email is sent and no calendar is changed. Permissions shown here are demo-specific. Inputs are kept in memory and cleared on reload.
